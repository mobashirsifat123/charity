IRWA WEBSITE SUMMARY

What it is:
IRWA is a charity and community website. People can donate to causes, read articles, read fatwas, ask questions, and join the newsletter.

How it developed:
It started as a simple charity crowdfunding site called ChariFund.
Later, it added better admin tools, online payments, and a stronger database/storage setup.
After that, it grew into IRWA, which now also includes articles, fatwas, bilingual support, and content management tools.
Current improvement work is focused on UX and speed by removing unnecessary waiting, reducing heavy front-end work, and making pages feel faster.
The platform is now also being restructured into a broader Dawah and Quran-learning product, with early work started on scholar roles, Quran progress tracking, and global audio playback.
The charity tools are expanding too, including a live-price Zakat calculator that can send users directly into Stripe checkout as a dedicated zakat payment flow.
Daily retention tools are being added as well, including a local prayer-times widget with browser geolocation, fallback city timings, countdown to the next prayer, and a simple Qibla compass.
The Dawah section is also expanding into structured learning, with early LMS foundations for scholar-led courses, video modules, classroom pages, and saved user progress.
The charity side is also becoming more transparent, with live donation pop-up notifications and a new impact dashboard showing totals, campaign progress, category breakdowns, and charts.
The admin panel is being reorganized too, with clearer sidebar groups for charity, dawah, learning, and community work, plus role-based access so scholars only see the parts they are meant to manage.
The Dawah admin tools are also getting richer, with new forms for scholar profiles, article creation, Supabase image uploads, and a richer article editor so content can be managed without changing code.
The learning admin side is expanding as well, with a real course builder that lets admins create course overviews, upload course covers, and keep adding, editing, or removing video modules over time.
The visual experience is also being polished with reusable micro-interactions like smoother card hover lift, soft color glow, and tactile button press effects to make the site feel more premium.
